1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document specifies, in business terms, what the Sky LIS product does: the two portals, their modules and pages, the data each page handles, the actions users can take, the rules the system enforces, and the end-to-end workflows that connect the pages. It is the reference for product owners, laboratory domain experts, designers, developers and testers, and the baseline for acceptance.

1.2 Scope

Sky LIS is a cloud, subscription-based, multi-tenant Laboratory Information System operated by National Technology. The Super Admin Portal serves National Technology staff; the Client Portal serves each subscribed laboratory (tenant) with its branches and staff. This SRS describes the full target product and marks the subset delivered in Release 1.

Out of scope:

  • Installation, hosting and infrastructure procedures.
  • Detailed visual design (the prototypes are the UI reference).
  • Quality control, outsourcing to external laboratories, SMS campaigns and inter-branch transfer modules beyond the placeholders noted here.
  • Country-specific regulatory reporting.

2. Business overview

2.1 Business context

National Technology replaces its on-premise LDM laboratory system with Sky LIS, a cloud product sold by subscription. A laboratory subscribes online, receives its own portal address and an independent copy of the global laboratory master data, and runs its daily operation — reception, samples, results, printing, money — from the Client Portal across its branches. National Technology operates the platform from the Super Admin Portal: client lifecycle, plans and wallets, global master data, libraries and platform configuration.

2.2 Business goals

  1. Let a laboratory start operating within a day of subscribing, with a ready catalogue and price lists.
  2. Preserve every operational safeguard laboratories rely on: duplicate-patient protection, the sample Received gate, verified-only printing, delta and panic alerts, contract-correct pricing, auditable money movements.
  3. Serve reception, bench, sign-off, accounts and administration personas from one bilingual, responsive workspace.
  4. Connect analysers and PCR instruments and release normal results automatically where the laboratory chooses to.
  5. Give National Technology central control of master data, subscriptions and support.

2.5 Release scope summary

Release 1 delivers the cross-cutting platform plus every page that existed in the first prototypes with its enhancements (Client Portal v2.1) and the complete operator portal except the integration library (Super Admin v3.1). Release 2 adds the new client pages and the integration, automation and quality-control modules. In Release 1 registration omits the hospital Location/Department and Room fields and captures the payment before saving; samples are received automatically on save because the sample pages arrive in Release 2.

3. System overview

3.1 Two portals, one platform

Super Admin Portal

National Technology operates the platform: clients, plans and wallets, admin users and roles, global master data, integration libraries, platform parameters, sessions and audit.

Client Portal

Each laboratory runs reception, samples, laboratory, finance, setup, integration, automation and account from its own portal URL, across its branches, in Arabic or English.

3.2 Cross-cutting capabilities

CapabilityDescription
Tenancy & branchesEvery record belongs to one tenant; users work in one branch per session; all transactions are branch-stamped. Global master data is copied to the tenant at creation and maintained independently afterwards (BR-25).
Bilingual, RTL, responsiveArabic and English on every screen with true right-to-left layout; master data carries Arabic names; documents print in the configured language; desktop, tablet and phone layouts.
SecurityUsername/password, OTP for recovery and credential changes, password policy, session timeout, lock and expiry, roles with per-screen Query/Insert/Update/Delete, special rights, result rights per clinical group and branch.
Audit & notificationsEvery create, update, delete, status change, print, payment, refund, transfer, cancellation and privilege change is logged with user, time, branch and device; a notification centre surfaces panic values, delays and agent status.
Grids & editorsSearch, status and type filters, Excel export, pagination, row actions; editors validate mandatory fields, block deletion of referenced records and prefer deactivation.
DocumentsBarcode labels (Code 128), job order, receipt, invoice, refund receipt, price quote, laboratory report with template, verifier signature and QR, abbreviated worksheet, worklist, cash-closing sheet, claim, analyser code sheet.
KeyboardCtrl+S saves the open form, Ctrl+K focuses search, F2 opens the service picker, Enter adds a typed service or shortcut, Esc closes dialogs.

6. Status models

Sample (tube)

Collected on scan at phlebotomy, Received on scan at the unit; auto-collect / auto-receive parameters set both on save. Results need Received (BR-03).

Test / result

A report unit takes the lowest status of its results; a profile advances only when every member has (BR-04). Only Verified prints (BR-09).

Visit

Completed when every report unit is printed and the balance is settled.

Payment balance

Remaining = patient share − payments + refunds (BR-14).

Home booking

Conversion creates the visit.

Claim

Issued claims are printed and sent to the payer.

Work-queue colour states

Nine colours per installation; the meaning is fixed.

8. Business rules

IDRuleStatement
BR-01Immutable identifiersPatient numbers are generated by the system and never change; accessions are sequential per visit; sample IDs are unique and never reused.
BR-02Tube mergeServices with the same clinical group, sample type and sample condition share one sample; any difference splits them.
BR-03Received gateA result may not be recorded until the sample status is Received (auto-receive emulates it in Release 1).
BR-04Profile completionA profile or package reaches Complete, Reviewed or Verified only when every member test has.
BR-05Reserved keeps the billDeferring a service keeps it in the visit and the bill with no sample; cancelling removes it from both.
BR-06Contract sets the pricePayer and contract tier determine every service's patient share and payer share.
BR-07Three-layer pricingBase list price × tier factors = contract price; Apply propagates base-list changes to all derived tiers except excluded services.
BR-08Patient condition selects the rangeReference ranges resolve by sex, age and patient condition.
BR-09Verify is a signatureVerification records who signed and when; only verified results print.
BR-10Not-X queue logic"Not X" returns everything below X in the test track.
BR-11Delta check warnsA delta flag must be investigated and acknowledged but does not block verification.
BR-12Frequency checkA repeat order inside the configured interval alerts or is prevented.
BR-13Turnaround clockExpected result = collection + duration counting only test days; delay colours are a percentage of the duration consumed.
BR-14Negative balance means refundA negative visit balance is returned through the refund flow, never above the refundable amount.
BR-15Nothing is erasedCancellations, transfers, rejections and edits keep the original data with reason, user and time.
BR-16Drawer equationExpected cash = cash payments − refunds; card and transfer amounts are not in the drawer.
BR-17Group privileges propagateChanging a role changes every user in it; changing one user affects that user only.
BR-18New sample, new barcodeA replacement sample carries a new barcode unless the tenant policy allows reinstatement.
BR-19Default list value is not a resultThe default of a list test is empty or a placeholder, never a value that reads as a result.
BR-20Hidden helpersHelper tests feeding formulas or sums never print.
BR-21Percentage tolerance is a multiplicationA ±20 % tolerance is written as × 0.8 and × 1.2 of the live range values.
BR-22Every boundary needs its complementA rule for one side of a boundary needs a condition or complement so other patients are not stopped.
BR-23No last result must not stopRules using the previous result must not stop patients who have none in the window.
BR-24Validated selection onlyFields that reference master data accept only selected values.
BR-25Tenant copy of master dataA tenant's master data is an independent copy of the global templates; later global changes follow the update policy.
BR-26Payment inside registration (Release 1)The amount captured on the registration page is recorded with the visit on save; it may not exceed the amount due; non-cash payments need a reference.
BR-27Treasury per device (withdrawn)Withdrawn on 2026-10-20 (LIS-CR-004): a money movement is stamped with the cashier, the session branch, the payment method and its reference; no cash drawer or device approval is required.
BR-28Sign-in by addressEach laboratory signs in only at its own address (<base>/lab/<slug>) and the Super Admin only at <base>/admin/login; a user who is not a member of the laboratory of the address, or not platform staff at the admin address, is refused exactly like a wrong password. After sign-in the session carries the laboratory; signing out or timing out returns to the same address.
