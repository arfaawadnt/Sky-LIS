2. Client portal — the 17 points

C-01

Bookmark the laboratory's address on the first visit

quick changeS
Find your lab >>> on the first time, ask if we want to add to bookmarks in the browser.
TodaySince v0.18.0 every laboratory has its own sign-in address …/lab/<slug>; the bare address shows "Find your laboratory". Nothing prompts the user to keep the address.
AnswerAgreed, with one technical limit: a web page cannot add a bookmark by itself — browsers forbid it. The page can prompt and guide, and it can remember the laboratory on this device.
How the application will deal with it
  • After the first successful sign-in from a "found" address, the laboratory sign-in page shows a one-time strip: "Save this address: press Ctrl+D (⌘+D on Mac) to bookmark it, or copy it" with a Copy address button and a "Don't show again" choice. The strip is dismissed per device (a cookie flag, no web storage in the SPA).
  • The "Find your laboratory" page lists the laboratories recently signed into on this device (slug + name) so the user clicks instead of typing; the list is a per-device cookie, never a user record.
  • The laboratory's address is also printed on the welcome SMS / e-mail of the subscription (notification code portal-url, already exists) and on SA-04 Client.
C-02

"Collected outside the laboratory" per sample, not per visit

change requestM · CR-008
Sample collected outside the lab should be on sample level not visit level.
TodayThe flag is one of the visit flags (Urgent · Biohazard · Sample collected outside the laboratory) on CP-06 and is stored on the visit (Visit.CollectedOutside), as in LDM.
AnswerAgreed: a visit with blood drawn in the laboratory and a urine cup brought from home is the common case. The flag moves to the tube (sample) and the visit keeps a derived "any sample collected outside" for lists and reports.
How the application will deal with it
  • Data: VisitTube.CollectedOutside (new column, migration; the old visit flag is copied to every tube of the visits that carry it, then kept read-only as history).
  • Registration (CP-06): the visit chip becomes a per-sample toggle inside the sample list that appears once services are entered (one row per tube: sample type, colour, "Collected outside" switch). A quick action "All outside" keeps the one-click case.
  • Samples: a tube marked outside skips the collection step (it is Collected on save with the time typed by the clerk, optional) and still passes the Received gate; the label prints "EXT".
  • Reports and tracker: the report shows the collection time per sample as today; the Lab Tracker shows an "ext" tag on the samples cell.
  • API: POST /api/v1/registration/visits takes samples[].collectedOutside; the old visit-level field stays accepted for one release and marks every tube.

A-03

How subscriptions & wallets work

explained
Need explanation how does it work.
The model as built (SA-05, intake PLT-001)
  • Plan (tab Plans): code, name, monthly fee in the plan's currency, limits (branches, users, analysers) and an SMS quota per month. Every client is on one plan.
  • Wallet (tab Client wallets): a prepaid balance per client in its currency. Top up adds credit (reference and note kept, every transaction listed and exportable).
  • Charging: once per period (monthly), the wallet is debited the plan's monthly fee — automatically by the charge job (hourly check, each client charged once per period) or at once with Run charges now. "Next charge" shows the date.
  • Trial: a client created through Subscribe starts in Trial for the configured days and is not charged; activating it (or the trial ending) makes it Active and chargeable. A client made inactive is not charged.
  • Balance at or below zero: the laboratory keeps working, but metered services pause (today: SMS beyond the quota is not sent). The dashboard tile "Wallets at or below zero" lists them; the reminder policy of C-16 will warn before it happens.
  • SMS used: counts the messages of the month against the plan quota; per-message pricing beyond the quota is not built because the price is not yet decided (business point Q12 of PLT-001).

The screenshot shows a client with balance 0.00, monthly fee 222.00 EGP and next charge 28/10: on that day the charge job will try to debit 222.00; with no top-up the wallet goes negative and metered services stay paused. A one-page "How billing works" help card will be added to the page header (and to the laboratory's Subscription parameters group) with this text in both languages.
