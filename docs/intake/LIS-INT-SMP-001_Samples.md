## 1. Purpose and policy basis

Every tube a visit needs is a **sample** with a unique, never-reused barcode (BR-01; LDM §2.1 "Sample ID"). The sample
carries the **physical track** of the tube — Ordered → Collected (scanned at phlebotomy) → Received (scanned at the
unit) — independently of the medical track of its tests (LDM §2.3). The **Received gate** is the control that protects
results: a result may not be recorded before the tube is Received (BR-03); laboratories that do not scan emulate the
gate with the auto-collect / auto-receive parameters (LDM §2.3 "Auto Receive"; Release 1 runs with both on). An unfit
tube is **rejected with a reason** so that no result is entered on it and reception recollects; it is then either
reinstated with the same barcode or replaced by a new sample with a new barcode, according to the tenant policy
(BR-18; LDM §5.9: "road 1 Unreject … road 2 (ISO-preferred) new sample"). Nothing is erased: every step is kept with
user, time and branch (BR-15) and shown as the tube's timeline (CP-17; LDM §6 "S. T. Sample Track").

## 2. Scope and out of scope

In scope:

- Creation of one sample per tube when a visit is registered or lines are added (policy on the Registration events,
  MODULES.md §3), applying the auto-collect / auto-receive parameters.
- Collect and receive by barcode scan, one tube at a time or every tube of an accession (CP-14).
- Samples of a visit with label printing (one / all; label sizes 50×25 and 38×25 mm; copies) and the job order link (CP-15).
- Reject with a catalogue reason and a note, unreject, replace with a new barcode; the list of rejected tubes (CP-16).
- Cancellation of a tube when its last live line is cancelled (policy on `VisitLineCancelled`, CP-26 "related samples cancelled").
- Tracking: the timeline of a tube with the time between stages (CP-17).
- Read port `ISampleStatusLookup` for Results (wave 3) to enforce BR-03; the query of today's rejected samples for the
  dashboard (CP-05 "rejected samples").

Out of scope (later intakes): sample storage and disposal (LDM "Sample Storage", undocumented), inter-branch
transport manifests, test round times on the tracking page (they are Results data: complete/reviewed/verified/print
dates, CP-17 "test round times" — added when Results exists), analyser sample codes (CP-60, Integration), notification
channels (the rejection event is the hook; the dashboard query is the first consumer).

## 5. Aggregates

- **Sample** (root, tenant-scoped, `app.samples`): `TubeId`, `Generation`, `SampleNumber`, `VisitId`, `Accession`,
  `PatientId`, `BranchId` (registering branch), `ClinicalGroupId`, `SampleTypeId`, `SampleConditionId?`,
  `ServiceIds[]` (the services on the tube), `Status`, `StatusChangedAtUtc`, collection (`CollectedAtUtc`,
  `CollectedByUserId`, `CollectedBranchId`), receipt (`ReceivedAtUtc`, `ReceivedByUserId`, `ReceivedBranchId`),
  `Rejection` (value object: `ReasonId`, reason names en/ar as of the rejection, `Note`, `AtUtc`, `ByUserId`, `BranchId`),
  `ReplacedBySampleId?`, `ReplacesSampleId?`, `CancelledAtUtc?`, `CancelledByUserId?`, `CancellationReason?`, `RowVersion` (xmin).
- **SampleEvent** (child entity of Sample, `app.sample_events`, key `(tenant_id, id)`, composite FK `(tenant_id, sample_id)`):
  `Action` (Ordered, Collected, Received, Rejected, Unrejected, Replaced, ReplacementOrdered, Cancelled), resulting `Status`,
  `AtUtc`, `ByUserId?`, `BranchId?`, `ReasonId?`, `Note?`. Appended by every transition, never changed (BR-15).

Other aggregates are referenced by id only: Visit, visit lines and the tube (Registration), Patient, Branch, catalogue
items, Reason.

## 6. State table

| State | Meaning | Editable fields | Frozen | Terminal | Results allowed (BR-03) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordered | tube expected, not drawn yet | none (only transitions) | no | no | no |
| Collected | drawn; time, user and branch recorded | none | no | no | no |
| Received | arrived at the unit | none | no | no | **yes** |
| Rejected | unfit, with reason; reception must recollect | none | no | no | no |
| Cancelled | no live line remains on the tube | none | yes | yes | no |
| Replaced | superseded by a new sample with a new barcode | none | yes | yes | no |

A sample is **live** unless it is Replaced. At most one live sample exists per tube.

## 7. Transition table

| # | From → To | Action | Actor (permission) | Preconditions (codes) | SoD | Gate | Reason | Events | System? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | — → Ordered (→ Collected → Received per settings) | Create from tube | policy P1 (`VisitRegistered`, `VisitLinesAdded`) | tube id (SMP-001), number (SMP-002), references (SMP-003); idempotent on `TubeId`; a tube all of whose lines are Reserved gets no sample (BR-05) | — | — | — | (`SampleCollected`, `SampleReceived` when the settings apply) | yes |
| T1b | (any live state, unchanged) | Add services | policy P1 (`VisitLinesAdded`) | the tube already has its sample; new lines on it (BR-02 merge on a saved visit); a Cancelled or Replaced sample takes none | — | — | — | — (field history) | yes |
| T2 | Ordered → Collected | Collect (scan in Collect mode; collect all) | `samples.create` | state Ordered (SMP-010); branch selected (TENANT-004) | — | — | — | `SampleCollected` (+ T3 when `autoReceive`) | no |
| T3 | Ordered / Collected → Received | Receive (scan in Receive mode; receive all) | `samples.create` | state Ordered or Collected (SMP-011); from Ordered the collection is recorded first, same user, branch and time | — | — | — | (`SampleCollected`) + `SampleReceived` | no |
| T4 | Collected / Received → Rejected | Reject | `samples.edit` | state (SMP-012); reason id present (SMP-020) and, when the catalogue is installed, an active reason of kind Rejection (SMP-021); note ≤ 500 | — | — | **required** (catalogue reason + optional note) | `SampleRejected` | no |

Bulk actions of an accession (CP-14 "collect/receive all samples of an accession") apply T2 / T3 to every live sample
of the visit for which the transition is allowed and report the others as unchanged; a visit without samples is SMP-405.

| INV-SMP-005 | Cancelled and Replaced are terminal; nothing changes them | aggregate state guards | SMP-010 … SMP-014 | "'x' is not allowed while the sample is in state 'y'." |

## 9. Segregation-of-duties rules

None. The SRS and LDM assign collect, receive, reject and reinstate to the same bench roles and give no four-eyes rule
for the physical track (the signature rules live on results, BR-09). Every step records the actor, so a later rule
(for example "the rejecter may not reinstate") can be added as `SOD-SMP-001` without a data change (§16 Q6).

## 10. Approval gates

None. Rejection is a recorded decision with a mandatory reason, not an approval; it is reversible by T5 / T6.

## 11. Events and policies

```text
Event:    SampleCollected { SampleId, SampleNumber, TubeId, VisitId, Accession, BranchId, ActorUserId }
Raised by: T1 (auto-collect), T2, T3 from Ordered, T6 (new sample, auto-collect)
Reacts:   Results — worklist collection time and the turnaround clock start (BR-13)   [wave 3, read through ISampleStatusLookup]

Event:    SampleReceived { SampleId, SampleNumber, TubeId, VisitId, Accession, BranchId, ActorUserId }
Raised by: T1 (auto-receive), T3, T6 (new sample, auto-receive)
Reacts:   Results — the Received gate opens (BR-03); Results reads the status through ISampleStatusLookup at entry time

Event:    SampleRejected { SampleId, SampleNumber, TubeId, VisitId, Accession, PatientId, RegisteredBranchId, BranchId, ReasonId, Reason, ActorUserId }
Raised by: T4
Reacts:   Reception notification (CP-16 "notifies reception"): the dashboard query GET /api/v1/samples/rejected-today; a
          Notifications policy can subscribe later (idempotent on SampleId + OccurredAtUtc)

Event:    SampleUnrejected { SampleId, SampleNumber, TubeId, VisitId, Accession, ActorUserId, Comment }   Raised by: T5
Event:    SampleReplaced  { OldSampleId, NewSampleId, NewSampleNumber, TubeId, OldSampleNumber, VisitId, Accession, ActorUserId, Comment }   Raised by: T6
          Reacts: Results — results stay on the tube (TubeId); the new number is shown on the worklist
Event:    SampleCancelled { SampleId, SampleNumber, TubeId, VisitId, Accession, ActorUserId, Reason }   Raised by: T7

Policy P1: when VisitRegistered or VisitLinesAdded, then create one Sample per tube (idempotent on TubeId), Ordered,
           (a tube that already has its live sample gains the services of the new lines, T1b),
           then Collected / Received by the registering user at the visit's branch at the event time when
           autoCollect / autoReceive are on. Skips a tube whose referencing lines are all Reserved (BR-05).
Policy P2: when VisitLineCancelled with a TubeId, then cancel the tube's live sample when no other line of the tube is
           Ordered or Reserved in app.visit_lines (raw SQL on the wave-2 table contract, MODULES.md §5). Idempotent.
```

Events go to `ops.outbox_events` in the same commit as the change; the outbox stamps the tenant. The audit reason of
a transition is taken from the event's `Reason` / `Comment`.

## 13. Reference data and configuration

Tenant parameters (`ITenantSettings`, keys in `SettingKeys`, edited on CP-57): `autoCollect` (default true),
`autoReceive` (default true; "marked Received when collected": also applies to a manual collect scan),
`newBarcodeOnReject` (default true: unreject refused, replacement required), `labelCopies` (1–10).
Reference data read by id: rejection reasons (`app.reasons`, kind Rejection), clinical groups (unit letter), sample
types and conditions, services (names on labels), patients (number, names), branches (names on the timeline). Names
are resolved at read time by raw SQL on the agreed tables; when a table is not installed yet, ids are returned and
the screen shows the id.

## 15. Notifications and escalations

Rejection notifies reception (CP-16): the `SampleRejected` event is raised and `GET /api/v1/samples/rejected-today`
feeds the dashboard tile and list (CP-05). No SMS / e-mail in this version (§16 Q7).

Open questions (assumed answers let the build proceed; confirm or change):

| # | Question | Assumed answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | BR-18 with `newBarcodeOnReject = true`: is unreject forbidden, or only discouraged? | Forbidden (SMP-016); replacement is the only road. With `false` both unreject (same barcode) and replacement are offered. |
| Q2 | Does `autoReceive` apply to manual collect scans? | Yes ("marked Received when collected", SettingKeys); a laboratory that scans at the unit turns it off. `autoReceive` without `autoCollect` still records both steps at creation. |
| Q3 | Which time and user are recorded by auto-collect / auto-receive? | The registering user, at the visit's branch, at the registration time (the event time), not the time the policy runs. |
| Q4 | Does a tube with only Reserved lines get a sample? | No (BR-05: "no sample"); it is created when Registration releases the line (`VisitLinesAdded`). A tube no line references is still created (defensive). |
| Q5 | May a tube be received in a branch other than the one that registered it? | Yes; every step records its own branch (transport between branches is normal). Lists filter on the registering branch. |
| Q6 | Is there a segregation-of-duties rule on reject / reinstate? | No (§9). |
| Q7 | How is reception "notified" of a rejection? | Event + dashboard query of today's rejections; SMS/e-mail later through Notifications. |
| Q8 | What does "today" mean for the dashboard? | The caller's local day: the client passes `utcOffsetMinutes`; default UTC. A tenant time zone parameter would replace it. |
| Q9 | Label copies | `max(labelCopies, highest label count of the tube's services)` when the services table carries `label_count`; else `labelCopies`. |
| Q10 | Label date/time | The collection time when collected, else the time the sample was ordered. |
| Q11 | Is a rejected sample cancelled when its last line is cancelled? | Yes (T7 from Rejected). A Replaced sample is never touched; the live replacement is cancelled instead. |

| Q13 | Retention of sample history | With the patient's laboratory record (10 years). |
