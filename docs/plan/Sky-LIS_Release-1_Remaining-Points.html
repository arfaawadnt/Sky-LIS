1. Summary

Everything inside the Release-1 scope (the pages of the v2.1 client and v3.1 admin prototype builds) is built, walked through, deployed and released. What remains falls into five phases: decisions that unblock the pilot, the pilot's own prerequisites, hardening of what exists, completion of the acceptance evidence, and the parked Release-2 modules.
